Install FocusForce+

Two steps, on your Android phone. Free, and nothing to sign up for.

On a computer? Open this page on your phone: karos555.github.io/FocusForcePlus/obtainium.html

  1. Get Obtainium

    A free, open source app. It installs FocusForce+ straight from GitHub and keeps it up to date.

    Download Obtainium

    Open the downloaded file to install it. Also on F-Droid and on its own page.

  2. Add FocusForce+

    Add to Obtainium

    Nothing happened? Then Obtainium is not on this phone yet. Do step 1 first.

    Obtainium opens. Tap Continue, then FocusForce+, then Install.

  3. Open FocusForce+

    The setup guide asks for its permissions one by one and says what each is for.

Why Obtainium?
  • Play Protect lets it through. Obtainium installs the way an app store does, so the block described below does not come.
  • Updates come by themselves. FocusForce+ has no internet access and cannot look for updates. Obtainium does, and installs them.
Adding it to Obtainium by hand
  1. In Obtainium, tap Add.
  2. Paste https://github.com/karOS555/FocusForcePlus as the app source URL.
  3. Switch on Include prereleases. FocusForce+ is in beta, so every version is a prerelease, and without the switch Obtainium finds none. The button above sets it for you.
  4. Tap Add, then Install.
Without Obtainium: the plain APK

Download the .apk file of the newest release, at the top of the list, and open it.

Open the releases

Play Protect blocks this on many phones. The message reads "App blocked to protect your device" and has no "Install anyway". It is a false alarm: Google blocks every app from outside a store that has an accessibility service, and the app blocker needs one. It says nothing about this app. FocusForce+ is open source and has no internet permission, so nothing can leave your phone.

The simple way around is Obtainium, above. If you want the plain APK anyway:

  1. Pause the scan for the install. Play Store, your profile picture, Play Protect, the gear, then switch off "Scan apps with Play Protect". Install the APK and switch the scan back on right away.
  2. Or install over USB with adb install from a computer. Play Protect stays on.

Check your download first: a SHA256SUMS.txt is attached to every release. An update by hand can run into the same block.
