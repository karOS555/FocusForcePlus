# FocusForce+

### The all-in-one app for ADHD brains, and anyone who wants more structure

Routines, todos, lists, an app blocker, focus and sleep in one Android app.<br/>With reminders you can't just swipe away.

<h2 id="in-short"><sub><img src="docs/readme/icons/lightbulb.svg" height="24" width="24" alt=""></sub> In short</h2>

- Routines, todos, lists, an app blocker, a focus timer and a sleep mode in **one app**. They work together: a routine can block your apps while it runs, and a todo can bring your shopping list along.
- It **doesn't let you off easily**. Reminders come back, and before you snooze, skip or quit early there is a small task to do first, like a sum.
- Everything **stays on your phone**. There is no account, no cloud and no ads, and the app doesn't have the internet permission.
- It is **free and open source**. There is nothing to buy and no subscription.

> [!NOTE]
> FocusForce+ is in public beta. If something breaks or feels wrong on your phone, please [open an issue](../../issues).

<br/>

<h2 id="why-i-built-it"><sub><img src="docs/readme/icons/user-round.svg" height="24" width="24" alt=""></sub> Why I built it</h2>

I built FocusForce+ for myself. Every routine app, todo app and blocker I tried stopped working the moment my motivation dipped, because I could swipe everything away, snooze forever, or just switch it off in two taps.

So I wrote the app I needed, and I have used it every day since.

<br/>

<h2 id="how-it-holds-you-to-it"><sub><img src="docs/readme/icons/lock-keyhole.svg" height="24" width="24" alt=""></sub> How it holds you to it</h2>

Most apps let you swipe, snooze or switch off. In FocusForce+ every way out has a small hurdle in front of it.

- **Speed bumps** come before you snooze, skip, quit early or pause a block. The app can ask again, make you wait, or hand you a small task: hold a button, drag a slider, trace a path or solve a sum.
- **Blocking follows your day.** When you start a routine or a focus session, your apps close with it. Sleep time and calendar events do the same.
- **Invincible mode** locks a single routine, focus session or block until it is over. The rest of your phone stays free.
- Reminders come back. If you swipe an important todo away it returns, and a high-priority one rings again every few minutes.
- **Tamper Protection** is optional. With it on, you can only loosen your locks during a short daily window, at a time you pick.

<tr>

You decide how strict all of this is. Every speed bump can be changed, for each action and even for a single todo or routine, and one switch turns them all off.

It also isn't meant as a trap. A speed bump takes a minute at most, no alarm rings forever, and you can uninstall the app in Android's settings at any time. The idea is to stop the reflex, not to lock you out of your phone.

<h2 id="who-it-is-for"><sub><img src="docs/readme/icons/users.svg" height="24" width="24" alt=""></sub> Who it is for</h2>

ADHD is what the app was built around. But you don't need ADHD, or any label, to get something out of it.

- If you **have ADHD**, it gives you structure from outside: alarms that start things, one step at a time, and reminders that don't give up.
- If you **keep putting things off**, "later" gets harder. Todos ring again, and snoozing takes a small task first.
- If you **lose hours to your phone**, there are limits, schedules and blocks that are not switched off in two taps.
- If you **just want a day that runs**, it is also simply a routine timer, a todo list, a shopping list and a focus timer in one app. All the strict parts are optional.

<h2 id="todos"><sub><img src="docs/readme/icons/circle-check-big.svg" height="24" width="24" alt=""></sub> Todos</h2>

A todo gets louder with its priority, so the important ones can't quietly sink in a list.

- **Low:** one quiet notification.
- **Medium:** it comes up on screen and comes back a couple of times.
- **High:** it rings like an alarm, and again every few minutes. Ticking it off takes a confirmation and a swipe along a path.

There is more to them:

- A todo without a date still comes back, at set times each day.
- Checklists inside a todo, repeating todos, and a trash for mistakes.
- A todo can carry a list. When it rings, your shopping list is right there to tick off.
- You can share text from any other app to make a todo, and keep todos in step with your calendar and your tasks app.

<h2 id="routines"><sub><img src="docs/readme/icons/alarm-clock.svg" height="24" width="24" alt=""></sub> Routines</h2>

A routine is a list of steps, and each step has its own timer.

- It starts with a real alarm, full screen, like an alarm clock.
- You go through it one step at a time. If you run over, the timer keeps counting and reminds you.
- Snoozing costs a sum, and you decide how many snoozes each routine gets.
- While it runs, it can block apps and switch on Do Not Disturb.
- A step can carry a list, like a packing list for "Pack the bag".
- You can duplicate a routine, or copy steps over from another one.

<h2 id="lists"><sub><img src="docs/readme/icons/list-checks.svg" height="24" width="24" alt=""></sub> Lists</h2>

There are two kinds, because shopping and packing work differently.

**Shopping list**

- You can type several things at once: `milk, 2 bread, flour 500 g`.
- It sorts itself by aisle and knows common groceries in English and German.
- What you bought before is one tap away, under "Buy again".
- For the shop there is [a view](docs/readme/shots/shopping.png) that shows only what is left, in big rows, and keeps the screen on.

**Checklist**

- It is for the same things every time, like packing or leaving the house.
- Ticked items stay where they are, and the top shows how far along you are.
- "Start over" resets it for the next trip.

Both kinds can remind you with a todo, be shared as text, and undo what you removed.

<h2 id="blocker"><sub><img src="docs/readme/icons/ban.svg" height="24" width="24" alt=""></sub> Blocker</h2>

The blocker closes the apps you pick:

- always, after a **daily limit**, or on a **schedule**, overnight too
- while a routine, a focus session or a calendar event is on
- with **one shared limit** for several apps, like 60 minutes for all social media
- or only **Reels and Shorts**, right away or after a daily time, while the rest of Instagram and YouTube keeps working

TikTok and other apps that are nothing but short videos show up on their own, and are blocked as a whole.

If you really need an app, "5 more minutes" is there a couple of times a day, after a short wait.

<h2 id="focus-and-sleep"><sub><img src="docs/readme/icons/moon.svg" height="24" width="24" alt=""></sub> Focus and sleep</h2>

- **Focus** silences the phone and blocks apps for a set time. If you want to end early, it asks first and then makes you wait.
- **Sleep** reminds you to wind down, keeps the night quiet and closes apps until morning. It can start a routine at bedtime and another one when you wake up.

<h2 id="home-statistics-widgets-and-themes"><sub><img src="docs/readme/icons/house.svg" height="24" width="24" alt=""></sub> Home, statistics, widgets and themes</h2>

- **Home** answers one question: what now? It shows how much of today is done and what is up next, with Start or Done right there. Below that, routines, todos and calendar events sit in one list.
- **Statistics** compare your last seven days with the seven before: focus time, routines, todos and screen time, with streaks.
- **Widgets** put today, quick focus and a list on your home screen.
- **Themes:** six ready-made ones, two of them light, or your own colours. Aero is a real Frutiger Aero style, with glass cards and liquid glass buttons over a summer sky, a green meadow and soap bubbles.

<h2 id="and-more"><sub><img src="docs/readme/icons/plug.svg" height="24" width="24" alt=""></sub> And more</h2>

- You choose which modules sit in the bottom bar and in which order, and you can switch a module off completely.
- **Calendar:** today's events on Home, todos with a date in a calendar you pick, and blocking during events.
- **Tasks apps:** two-way sync with OpenTasks or Tasks.org. Through DAVx5 that reaches Nextcloud and any other CalDAV server.
- Share text from any app to make a todo or list items.
- `focusforceplus://` links start focus or a routine from Tasker, shortcuts or NFC tags. They are off until you switch them on.
- **Backups** go into a folder you choose every night, or into a single file by hand. An import can be undone.

All of this stays on your phone. There is no account and no cloud, and the app has no internet permission.

<h2 id="install"><sub><img src="docs/readme/icons/download.svg" height="24" width="24" alt=""></sub> Install</h2>

FocusForce+ needs Android 8 or newer. It isn't in an app store yet.

<h3 id="install-with-obtainium">The easy way: Obtainium</h3>

Open **[the install page](https://karos555.github.io/FocusForcePlus/obtainium.html)** on your phone. It has a button for each of the two steps:

1. Get **Obtainium**, a free app that installs FocusForce+ straight from GitHub.
2. Add **FocusForce+** to it and tap Install.

That's all. Obtainium also brings every update, and Play Protect lets the install through.

<h3 id="install-the-apk">Without Obtainium: the plain APK</h3>

Download the `.apk` from [the newest release](https://github.com/karOS555/FocusForcePlus/releases) and open it on your phone.

> [!WARNING]
> Play Protect blocks the plain APK on many phones, with the message "App blocked to protect your device". That is a false alarm: Google blocks every app from outside a store that has an accessibility service, and the app blocker needs one. With Obtainium the block doesn't come. [What else works](https://karos555.github.io/FocusForcePlus/obtainium.html#apk)

<h3 id="updates">After installing</h3>

- The setup guide asks for the permissions one by one and says what each is for.
- Updates come through Obtainium. Without it, **Settings > About > Check for updates** opens the releases page, because the app itself has no internet access.

<summary><sub><img src="docs/readme/icons/info.svg" height="18" width="18" alt=""></sub> <b>Privacy</b></summary>

<br/>

The app collects nothing and sends nothing. There is no analytics, no crash reporting and no ad SDK, and it has no internet permission.

Each permission belongs to one feature:

- **Accessibility, for the blocker:** it only learns which app you opened and reads nothing on the screen.
- **Accessibility, for Reels and Shorts** (optional): it only works inside Instagram and YouTube, and only looks for their short-video player.
- **Usage access** measures app time for limits and statistics.
- **Do Not Disturb access** is for focus, sleep and routines.
- **Calendar and tasks** (optional): only the calendar and the list you pick.

All details are in [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

</details>
